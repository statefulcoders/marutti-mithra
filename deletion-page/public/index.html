How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the app. Follow these steps:

  1. Open the app and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Select the Delete Account option.
  3. A confirmation dialog will appear with Cancel and Delete buttons.
  4. Confirm by selecting Delete to permanently remove your account and data.
  5. If you have not logged in recently, the app will prompt you to reauthenticate before you can delete your account. After re-login, repeat the steps above.

Note: Account deletion is irreversible. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
